What We Offer

Choose the Right Fit

Group Classes

$25 per class · hour

Small-group instruction organized by grade level and subject — every grade gets a dedicated Reading/Writing class and a dedicated Math class each week. Classes are capped at six students so every child gets real attention, and the curriculum is built around the next-grade-level standards your child will be expected to meet. Pick the grade and subject and we run the schedule; see the full weekly calendar below.

Grades 1–5 · Reading/Writing & Math · Max 6 students · 1-hour sessions

One-on-One Tutoring

$50 per hour

The most personalized way to learn. A credentialed educator works one-on-one with your child to build a plan around their specific strengths, gaps, and goals — and adjusts it session by session as they grow. Open to every subject and every grade, from kindergarten foundations through high school test prep, with scheduling flexible around your family.

Kindergarten – 12th Grade · All Subjects · Fully Personalized · Flexible Scheduling

Summer Camp

$100 per day

A full day of learning and play. Campers rotate through math, reading, art, and technology stations — keeping their minds sharp all summer while making friends and trying new things. Drop in for a single day or sign up for the full week. Snacks and water are provided throughout the day.

9:00 AM – 3:00 PM · Math, Reading, Art & Tech · Snacks Included · Drop-in or Weekly
